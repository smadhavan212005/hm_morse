Custom HM Morse Guide

hm
Represents a Morse dot ( . ). E.g., E = hm.
hmm
Represents a Morse dash ( - ). E.g., T = hmm.
Space
Separates dots and dashes within a single character. E.g., A (.-) = hm hmm.
Newline
Every character is printed on its own line. No blank lines within a word.

Separates individual words. Represents a standard space character.
